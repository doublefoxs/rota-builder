Rota Builder

Tell it how many people each shift needs and how many hours a week they should work. It finds how many staff you need, builds a repeating rota pattern, and shows what each person works.

How it works: every person follows the same repeating cycle but starts at a different point, so every day has the same cover. Hours per 7 days is the average over the whole cycle: any single week can run higher or lower because each morning or night that enters or leaves the week changes it. The pattern keeps a night shift followed by a day off and avoids a morning straight into a night, then keeps working runs short. It assumes mornings end in the evening and nights start in the evening and end next morning.
